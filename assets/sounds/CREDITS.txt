2DSAGE - sound credits
======================
One entry per sound file: file - title by author (source), link - licence.
Only CC0, CC-BY or own sounds belong in this public repository. Sounds under
licences that forbid redistributing the files themselves (e.g. Pixabay) must
not be committed here. CC-BY sounds must also be credited in the game itself.
All files were cut and/or converted to 16-bit 44.1 kHz WAV for the engine.
